\begin{abstract}
This public review copy defines the hardware-neutral interface between DUNE
Slow Controls (SC) and the Ground Impedance Monitor (GIZMo).  The versioned
\code{urn:fnal:gizmo} information model specified by this ICD is the
authoritative GIZMo--SC OPC~UA contract.  Any GIZMo hardware implementation
shall conform to it.  GIZMo Kria is the presently implemented reference, while
a server for legacy GIZMo on the ZedBoard is under development as an
independent conforming producer; neither implementation defines a separate
contract.  The document
separates the physical measurement layer, hardware-control layer, internal
application protocols, public OPC~UA interface, Ignition display and history,
Detector Protection boundary, calibration authority, and commissioning gates.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}

The GIZMo monitors the impedance relationship between detector ground and
building ground.  It applies an AC stimulus, observes the returned signal
through the installed current-transformer and analog path, calculates
impedance-related quantities, and provides a local alarm indication.  SC uses
the public machine interface for authorized configuration and operations,
operator display, alarms, and history.

The maintained GIZMo runtime (package version 0.4.6, OPC~UA model 1.3.1)
provides the Kria endpoint and is implementation evidence for the current
contract baseline.  The legacy GIZMo continues to run the preserved 2017 ARM
controller.  A target-native ZedBoard OPC~UA producer is under development
against this ICD; it is not represented here as deployed, accepted, or
available to production SC.  It shall be independently implemented and shall
not require source, configuration, service, or endpoint changes on the Kria.
The earlier monitoring-only adapter and direct read-only observations remain
validation evidence, not the production dependency.

A conforming ZedBoard implementation shall validate the controller executable
against a controlled SHA-256 fingerprint before publishing its data.  The
fingerprint is omitted from this public copy and retained in the commissioning
record:

\begin{lstlisting}
<controlled-executable-sha256>
\end{lstlisting}

This ICD specifies interfaces and ownership; it does not replace electrical
schematics, the device software design, a Detector Protection design, or a
traceable metrology procedure.  In particular, existence of legacy calibrator
hardware and a \code{CAL} command does not establish a production self-
calibration process.

\section{System Architecture}

\subsection{Layered architecture}

\begin{lstlisting}
Layer 0  Detector ground <-> building ground / CT / saturable inductor
                           |
Layer 1  GIZMo analog front end, ADC/DAC, FPGA, relays, local display
                           |
Layer 2  Platform controller and private application protocols
         Kria: ZMon :5055, ZMQ :5555, private control socket
         Zed:  GIZMO.elf, FPGA MMIO, VM801P SPI, SysV service
                           |
Layer 3  Public interface: OPC UA Binary over UA TCP
                           |
Layer 4  Ignition: tags, controls, alarms, trends, and history
\end{lstlisting}

Only Layer~3 is the normal SC command/data boundary.  Interfaces in Layer~2
are private implementation details and shall not become independent SC control
paths.  Layer~0 and Layer~1 work is a controlled electrical or calibration
activity.

\req{005} SC shall support both GIZMo Kria and legacy GIZMo through the common
OPC~UA and Ignition interface described in this ICD.  Any GIZMo hardware
implementation shall conform to this contract.  Platform-specific capability
differences shall be represented explicitly and shall not redefine, narrow, or
advance the canonical model.  This common interface does not assert that one
hardware implementation has superior metrology.

\req{006} Each hardware instance shall have a distinct endpoint,
\code{ApplicationUri}, certificate/trust identity, \code{DeviceId}, hostname,
and Ignition history path.  A ZedBoard producer shall not proxy, impersonate,
reuse the identity of, or become a required dependency of the Kria endpoint.

\req{007} Stopping, starting, upgrading, disconnecting, or failing the ZedBoard
OPC~UA producer shall not change Kria operation and shall not stop or perturb
the preserved ZedBoard controller, local display, stimulus, physical alarm, or
measurement cadence.  Monitoring shall not contend for FPGA/MMIO, display SPI,
relay, file, or process ownership.  Any future mutation requires an explicit
serialized ownership design and the gates in Section~\ref{sec:legacy-control}.

\section{Physical and Electrical Interface}

\subsection{Measurement boundary}

The installed measurement path consists of detector ground, building-ground
reference, the stimulus path, saturable-inductor assembly, current transformer
(CT), analog front end, and the associated cable shields and chassis bonding.
The system drawing places the GIZMo, CT, saturable inductor, alarm beacon, and
audio indication across the detector/building-ground monitoring boundary.

The legacy rear centers labelled \code{TO DETECTOR} and \code{FROM CT} are
active stimulus/output and signal/input conductors.  A powered resistance-mode
measurement across them injects meter current into the active loop and is not
a passive detector-ground measurement.  The observed 50-kohm external-box
response is useful characterization evidence but is not a calibration point.

\subsection{Kria hardware layer}

GIZMo Kria uses an AMD/Xilinx Kria K26 SOM mounted on the KR260 Robotics
Starter Kit carrier, the custom control board, FPGA overlay, ADC/DAC signal
path, relay drivers, 5-inch EVE display, alarm/audio connections, and two
Processing-System Ethernet interfaces.
The maintained software maps the installed FPGA resources at fixed addresses;
the overlay identity and expected devices are monitored through the public
model.

\req{013} The Kria overlay, control-board revision, calibration tables, and
physical cable assignment shall form one controlled device configuration.

\subsection{Legacy hardware layer}

The legacy platform consists of a ZedBoard, 2017 FPGA bitstream, amplifier
board 176962, PMod calibrator 176971, and VM801P/FT801 local display.  The
calibrator has five populated binary-weighted sections measured during
characterization as 8.15, 16.17, 31.60, 63.52, and 127.12~ohm.  The R6
footprint is unpopulated and participates in the high-impedance disconnect
topology.  These DMM readings are as-built engineering observations, not
traceable standards.

The relay controller accepts a one-shot set/reset pulse for each latching
relay.  Finite states select combinations of R1--R5; the restored normal state
opens the chain through the unpopulated R6 position.  Any diagnostic that owns
relay MMIO shall first stop \code{GIZMO.elf}, guarantee cleanup on normal exit,
error, and interruption, verify restoration, and then restart the original
controller.

\section{Link, Transport, and Internal Protocols}

\subsection{Controls network}

The SC-facing physical network is Ethernet.  Each OPC~UA server shall use a
site-assigned address on the restricted controls network.  Final hostname,
address, route, switch port, VLAN, and firewall entries are installation data.

\subsection{Kria private interfaces}

\begin{longtable}{L{0.20\textwidth} L{0.18\textwidth} L{0.52\textwidth}}

The package target \code{gizmo.target} is the local service-lifecycle boundary.
Systemd starts, stops, supervises, and restarts the owned components.  Service
state is monitored through OPC~UA; arbitrary \code{systemctl} execution is not
an OPC~UA capability.

\subsection{Legacy private interfaces}

The 2017 controller is launched by its SysV service in normal measurement
mode. Its command parser has native commands including
\code{RUN}, \code{CAL}, \code{set_th}, \code{read_adc}, \code{set_time},
\code{set_res}, and \code{set_rly}.  The SysV wrapper supports start, stop,
restart, and status.  These facts establish native capability; they do not by
themselves authorize remote SC control.

The Zed-to-VM801P display link is a private 16-bit SPI exchange at 4~MHz,
mode~0, MSB-first.  The controller sends a tagged threshold, tagged resistance,
and an untagged comparison value; the display returns alarm bits used by the
legacy controller.  This SPI link is not an SC interface.

The earlier off-board adapter demonstrated a recovery collector that reads
coherent completed \code{RUN} frames from the exact executable.  It checks one
PID, executable identity, coherent finite I/Q/magnitude/resistance, and the
recovered transfer function before publishing a new sequence.  Repeated polls
of the same frame do not manufacture new measurements.  A target-local
equivalent and bounded recovery journal are candidate implementation elements
for the ZedBoard producer; they are not represented as deployed or accepted by
this revision.

\section{Public OPC UA Application Interface}

\subsection{Normative information model and endpoint identity}

The public protocol is OPC~UA Binary over UA TCP.  Public-review endpoint forms
are:

\begin{lstlisting}
GIZMo Kria:   opc.tcp://<assigned-gizmo-host>:4840
Legacy GIZMo: opc.tcp://<assigned-legacy-host>:4842
\end{lstlisting}

The legacy form is a commissioning target, not a deployed endpoint.  Its host
shall be assigned independently and its use of TCP~4842 shall not change the
Kria endpoint on TCP~4840.

The namespace URI is \code{urn:fnal:gizmo}.  This ICD and its companion
contract artifacts are normative; a runtime or server is an implementation of
them.  Clients shall resolve the URI at connection time and shall not hard-code
a namespace index.  A shared namespace URI identifies a model, not a server
instance: all producers shall retain the distinct identities required by
\req{006}.  Canonical objects are rooted at \code{Objects/GIZMo}; deterministic
string NodeIds include:

\begin{lstlisting}
GIZMo.Identity.*
GIZMo.Measurement.*
GIZMo.Alarm.*
GIZMo.Configuration.*
GIZMo.Operations.*
GIZMo.Calibration.*
GIZMo.Health.*
\end{lstlisting}

The current released information-model baseline is version 1.3.1, published
13 August 2026.  Rows labelled ``REQUIRED MODEL 1.4'' in the intake workbooks are
forward requirements under review; they are not part of model 1.3.1 and shall
not be advertised under that version.  The workbooks are selected SC
integration profiles and do not create independent NodeIds or semantics.  If a
workbook entry and normative prose conflict, the conflict shall be resolved by
joint GIZMo--SC change control before implementation or commissioning.

The model-1.3.1 implementation inventory reviewed at GIZMo commit
\code{f9683cc} contains 43 objects, 457 variables, and five methods.  Its
generated inventory hash is:

\begin{lstlisting}
sha256=1f10f15a8991a758d7b0c59ef220002336a9615e3378401a6456668e86b51b97
\end{lstlisting}

That generated inventory is a reproducible conformance fixture and source of
implementation evidence.  It does not make the generator or a particular
hardware platform the interface authority.

Model 1.x revisions preserve NodeId, node class, datatype, rank, units, method
signature, and physical meaning.  A breaking change requires a new major-
version namespace.  A shared model revision requires joint GIZMo--SC ICD change
control; no hardware implementation may unilaterally publish one.  A platform-
specific extension, if ever required, shall use a different namespace and
shall not be required by SC.  The recovered
\code{SimpleOPCUAServer/CommandObject} tree may remain outside the SC boundary
for migration, but new SC integration shall use only the canonical model.

\req{016} A GIZMo producer claiming conformance shall match this contract's
canonical browse paths, NodeIds, node classes, datatypes, ranks, units,
EngineeringUnits/EURange metadata where defined, method signatures,
StatusCodes, and source-timestamp meanings for the model version it reports.
Its effective access shall be no more permissive than the contract and shall
reflect the authenticated role and advertised device capability.  A producer
shall not report a model version that has not been released through ICD change
control.

\req{017} Canonical nodes required by the producer's reported model version
shall remain discoverable when a hardware implementation cannot supply their
values.  Unsupported or unavailable data shall carry
\code{BadNotSupported}, \code{BadDataUnavailable}, or another specific non-good
StatusCode; unsupported methods shall fail with \code{BadNotSupported}.  A
platform limitation shall not be hidden by deleting, retyping, renaming, or
silently repurposing a canonical node.

\req{018} Address-space conformance does not grant control authority.  SC shall
enable a write or method only when this contract defines it, the producer
advertises the capability, the authenticated role permits it, and the operation
passes the separate security, serialization, non-interference, readback,
rollback, and commissioning gates in this ICD.  The initial ZedBoard
integration profile is monitoring-only.

\subsection{DataValue and quality semantics}

Every variable shall provide its OPC~UA datatype, StatusCode, and source
timestamp.  Unknown numeric values shall not be exposed as plausible zero.
Before a first sample, the status is \code{BadWaitingForInitialData}; a retained
sample after loss may be \code{UncertainLastUsableValue}; an unavailable
platform feature is \code{BadNotSupported} or \code{BadDataUnavailable}.

\req{020} \code{ResistanceOhm} is a physical value only when
\code{ResistanceRange=InRange}.  HIGH~Z shall be an explicit range state with
a null/NaN resistance and non-good quality, never a fabricated 500- or
999-ohm measurement.

\req{021} \code{Alarm.Active}, when available, shall come from the device's
authoritative alarm branch.  SC, the OPC~UA producer, and Ignition shall not recompute
it from the resistance and threshold readbacks.

\subsection{Monitoring groups}

The canonical model contains Identity, Measurement, Alarm, Thermal, Time,
OperatingSystem, Network, Storage, Firmware, Services, Calibration, SDR,
Configuration, Operations, and Health groups.  The two platform intake lists
are implementation-specific SC integration views of the same contract.  Rows
marked as current model 1.3.1 describe the released baseline; rows marked as
required model 1.4 are proposals until approved through ICD change control.
Unsupported fields remain present and carry a non-good StatusCode.  Platform-
private diagnostics and controls are not added to \code{urn:fnal:gizmo} by an
individual hardware implementation.

\subsection{Numeric-field convention}

In the companion intake spreadsheets, \emph{Sensor Span} is a verified
software bound or documented presentation domain, \emph{Nominal Value} is the
requested initial value or a clearly identified current observation, and
\emph{Desired Range} is the initial normal or commissioning check band.  A
wider authorized write range, where one exists, is stated in Notes.  The
Low/High, delay, deadband, and action
columns define Ignition alarm configuration only.  A blank cell means that no
supported number or action has been established; it is not zero and it is not
``not applicable'' data copied from the intake template.  Notes distinguish
implemented bounds, current device/package values, measured observations, and
provisional commissioning choices.

\subsection{GIZMo Kria controls}

GIZMo Kria exposes two writable UInt32 configuration variables.  Their stable
NodeIds and ranges are:

\begin{lstlisting}
GIZMo.Configuration.ThresholdOhm              0 .. 1023 ohm
GIZMo.Configuration.AveragesPerCalculation    1 .. 1,000,000
\end{lstlisting}

The Kria package initializes the threshold and averaging count to 100.  For
initial joint commissioning, SC shall request 100~ohm and verify the live
\code{Measurement.ThresholdOhm} readback.  This value matches the sustained
setpoint in the retained Kria history; a brief 2-kohm commissioning excursion
is test evidence and is not a desired operating value.  Although the firmware
accepts zero,
the initial Ignition operator range is 1--500~ohm because zero effectively
disables the resistance-threshold branch.  The accepted OPC~UA bounds remain
unchanged.

It exposes the following typed methods and command-result variable:

GIZMo.Operations.StartCalibration(UInt32)

GIZMo.Operations.NormalizeMagnitude()
GIZMo.Operations.SetSystemTime(DateTime)
GIZMo.Configuration.LastCommandResult
\end{lstlisting}

\req{030} SC writes and methods shall be bounded, role-authorized, logged, and
followed by readback of the resulting state.  A transport-level success alone
does not prove that the requested physical state was reached.

\req{031} Clearing the latch shall not suppress an active physical alarm.  A
device restart or reboot shall not be described as ``clear latch.''

\req{032} Restarting \code{gizmo-zmon.service} and rebooting the Kria are local
maintenance/recovery actions until separately specified as canonical OPC~UA
methods.  SC shall not infer those permissions from writable service-status
nodes; service-status nodes are read-only.

\subsection{Legacy GIZMo conformance and controlled expansion}\label{sec:legacy-control}

A target-native ARMv7 OPC~UA producer for the ZedBoard is under development.
This revision does not claim that the server, a target-local recovery journal,
an automatic time service, or an authenticated OPC~UA write has been deployed
or accepted.  Current evidence is limited to recovered engineering behavior,
direct read-only frames, and the off-board monitoring-adapter pilot described
below.

The initial server shall be an observational sidecar.  It shall consume only a
coherent completed-record boundary, preserve the controller's source time and
sequence, and survive its own stop, restart, client loss, and network loss
without signalling, pausing, replacing, or reconfiguring \code{GIZMO.elf}.
It shall not own or compete for FPGA/MMIO, VM801P SPI, relay, calibration, or
stimulus resources.  Server installation and removal shall require no Kria
source, service, configuration, or endpoint change.

The recovered executable has one threshold word, one persistent threshold
file, and a deterministic display update transaction, with a recovered native

write.  Any future threshold control shall first define contract-compliant
device-capability/range semantics through joint ICD change control, then
require fresh source state, exact controller identity, serialized ownership,
persistent/live/display readback, bounded rollback, audit, and physical-display
acceptance.  SC shall never pass arbitrary shell text, file paths, or raw MMIO
values to the target.

\section{Detector Control Display, Alarms, and History}

\subsection{Display}

Ignition shall show source identity, endpoint health, sample age, measurement
quality, resistance/range, threshold, calibration state, and relevant control
capabilities.  The hardware implementation shall be labelled \emph{GIZMo Kria
(K26/KR260)} or \emph{Legacy GIZMo (ZedBoard)} wherever it could be ambiguous.
HIGH~Z is rendered as a state, not as a precise endpoint.  The local display
remains an independent commissioning reference; disagreement creates a
diagnostic event and never triggers automatic calibration.

\subsection{Sampling and freshness}

Kria measurement data update nominally each second; platform data update at
10- or 30-second cadence. Clients should use subscriptions. The legacy recovery
collector may sample each second, but it emits a record only for a new coherent
completed \code{RUN} result. A conforming ZedBoard producer shall publish that
source sequence, and Ignition shall not manufacture intervening history
samples. A stale source becomes non-good within two expected completed cycles.

\subsection{Ignition history boundary}

Ignition shall subscribe directly to each GIZMo OPC~UA endpoint and shall own
the production tag history.  History shall preserve the reported device
identity, OPC~UA source timestamp, StatusCode, null/HIGH~Z gaps, and command
audit events.  The SC integration requires no intermediate history service
between OPC~UA and Ignition.  Ignition history failure shall not stop local
measurement or physical alarm indication.

\subsection{Alarms and Detector Protection}

SC shall alarm separately on physical ground-impedance alarm, communication
loss, stale data, non-good measurement quality, identity mismatch, and command
failure.  An unavailable legacy \code{Alarm.Active} remains unknown; it shall
not be converted to clear/false.

The Kria measurement engine contains no alarm on-delay or hysteresis in the
reviewed decision path.  The recovered legacy display comparison likewise has
no delay or hysteresis.  SC therefore adds neither: the Ignition on-delay is
0~s and the deadband field is blank.  Ignition shall alarm on the Boolean
\code{Alarm.Active}; it shall not create a second resistance comparator that
could disagree with the phase-aware Kria decision or the legacy rounded-value
decision.

The retained Kria SQLite replica covers 27 July through 3 August 2026 and
contains 560,762 fast samples, 56,082 platform samples, 9,457 minute rollups,
and 227 transition events.  It confirms a sustained 100-ohm threshold, a
stable 1436-Hz stimulus value, HIGH~Z as the predominant normal range state,
and both implemented alarm-reason branches.  Its steady-state distributions
are used to populate provisional Desired Range fields for temperatures,
utilization, available memory, phase, and lock-in counts.  Commissioning and
startup excursions are retained in Notes but are not promoted to desired
values.  The retained run was captured under model 1.3.0 and the then-installed
runtime 0.4.4; the maintained public source baseline reviewed for this ICD is
runtime 0.4.6 / model 1.3.1.

A read-only live check of the connected legacy board on 12 August 2026
collected 15 coherent completed \code{RUN} frames from the unchanged
\code{GIZMO.elf}.  The controller returned a 100-ohm threshold, 15
accumulations, 111.554--111.902~ohm calculated resistance, and a 112-ohm green
display.  The I, Q, magnitude, and derived \code{atan2} observations are
recorded in the legacy workbook as same-setup commissioning envelopes.  They
do not validate the missing CT/inductor path or the full detector-loop
calibration.

The deployed adapter subsequently captured 27 contiguous cycles
(\code{Sequence} 21--47) on 13 August 2026.  Resistance was
111.465703--112.010978~ohm (mean 111.722419~ohm), the threshold remained
exactly 100~ohm, and the calculated legacy display-equivalent rounding was
112~ohm in 26 cycles and 111~ohm in one.  Target communication was then lost
before 100 cycles.  The captured values carried
\code{UncertainLastUsableValue} and \code{Measurement.Quality=Uncertain},
consistent with the unvalidated legacy measurement path; after the loss, the
adapter exposed \code{BadNoCommunication} and \code{Health.Overall=Failed}
rather than fresh data.  This adds evidence for the attributes and loss
semantics but does not close \openitem{05}.

No target-native ZedBoard OPC~UA server or authenticated write is accepted as
commissioning evidence in this revision.  Its implementation, schema
conformance, non-interference behavior, security boundary, and physical-
display comparison remain open acceptance work.

No reviewed GIZMo or SC source establishes numerical temperature, CPU,
memory, storage, or similar supervisory alarm thresholds.  Observed envelopes
therefore remain monitor/trend checks and the spreadsheet alarm cells remain
intentionally blank.  Approved limits can be added without changing the
OPC~UA NodeIds.

Priority, response ownership, and any Detector Protection action still require
joint approval by the GIZMo owner, SC, DPS, and operations.  A required hard
protective action shall not depend only on Linux, OPC~UA, or Ignition.

\section{Calibration and Maintenance}

\subsection{GIZMo Kria}

GIZMo Kria calibration is a controlled maintenance method, not an automatic
SC reaction.  Production tables shall be staged, validated, content-hashed,
activated atomically, and rollback-tested.  Success, error, timeout, process
termination, and power-interruption paths shall restore and verify the normal
relay state.  Calibration state and the active table identities are published
through OPC~UA.

\subsection{Legacy GIZMo}

The legacy \code{CAL N} command selects 32 R1--R5 states and logs lock-in
measurements.  It does not fit, validate, persist, or activate a transfer
function; it does not use the abandoned \code{calib2.txt}; and it does not
guarantee normal-state restoration at completion.  Starting \code{RUN}
restores the normal \code{SetRes(-1)} selection, but an interrupted calibration
can leave a finite test resistance selected.

Accordingly, the initial endpoint reports
\code{Calibration.State=CharacterizationOnly} or \code{Unvalidated}.  A future
calibration workflow requires the restored CT/saturable-inductor fixture,
measured standards, a common runtime/calibration acquisition primitive, raw I/Q
and relay-state records, up/down sweeps, blind validation, clipping and
hysteresis checks, guaranteed cleanup, atomic model activation, and rollback.
Capacitance remains unsupported until mixed R/C characterization establishes a
defensible model.

\section{Security and Access Control}

Every GIZMo endpoint belongs on the restricted controls network or behind
approved authenticated transport.  Final certificates, trust lists,
usernames/roles, and firewall rules are installation data jointly assigned by
SC and site cybersecurity.

GIZMo Kria currently supports an explicitly configured insecure OPC~UA policy
for an isolated network; serialization is not encryption.  Production
deployment shall document whether that exception is accepted or deploy the
site certificate and role policy.

During development, the ZedBoard endpoint shall remain isolated from production
SC and untrusted networks.  A \code{SecurityPolicy None} or username/password
bench configuration does not protect credentials on the wire and is not
production acceptance.  Production credentials, if introduced, shall be
supplied to Ignition through its protected connection-credential store.
Unrestricted root SSH is maintenance access, not a production command
interface.  The producer shall not expose arbitrary command strings,
environment values, file paths, shell execution, general FPGA MMIO, or relay
bit patterns.

Credentials, private keys, and unbounded log text shall not be published as
OPC~UA values or Ignition history/audit events.

\section{Test Stand, Commissioning, and Acceptance}

\subsection{Physical and common interface acceptance}

Before SC sign-off, the parties shall:

\begin{itemize}
  \item verify connector labels, power, fusing, shield/ground assignment,
  CT polarity, and the approved stimulus path;
  \item resolve the namespace URI and verify every required NodeId, datatype,
  access class, EngineeringUnits value, StatusCode, and source timestamp with a
  generic OPC~UA client;
  \item verify DeviceId, platform, runtime/ELF identity, endpoint, and time;
  \item force disconnect, stale data, restart, and out-of-range conditions and
  confirm non-good quality and last-good semantics;
  \item confirm HIGH~Z is never persisted as a fabricated numeric value;
  \item verify Ignition display, alarm journal, command audit, trends, and
  history identity separation; and
  \item demonstrate that OPC~UA or Ignition-history failure does not stop local
  measurement or physical alarm indication.
\end{itemize}

The same versioned, machine-readable conformance checks shall be run against
every GIZMo producer.  They shall compare the namespace URI, browse paths,
NodeIds, node classes, datatypes, ranks, units/ranges, method signatures,
access/capability restrictions, quality behavior, source timestamps, and
instance identity against this ICD.  Passing those tests establishes interface
conformance only; it does not establish metrology or authorize controls.

\subsection{GIZMo Kria acceptance}

All maintained runtime tests shall pass.  Threshold and averaging writes shall
enforce type and range; every method shall report a bounded result.  Latch clear,
time correction, ADC capture, magnitude normalization, and calibration shall be
tested with authorization and readback.  Calibration testing shall include
error/interruption cleanup and rollback.  A 24-hour soak and power-interruption
recovery test shall complete before production approval.

\subsection{Legacy GIZMo monitoring acceptance}

At least 100 completed cycles shall match the physical display's rounded
resistance, including identical consecutive readings.  Wrong hash, multiple or
changed PID, target restart, server restart, network outage, partial update,
stale frame, and non-finite values shall not produce a false fresh sample.
Every write and method in the initial monitoring-only profile shall fail
explicitly. Ignition shall retain the ZedBoard samples with their reported
device identity, source timestamp, and StatusCode.  Start/stop, upgrade,
disconnect, load, and failure tests of the OPC~UA producer shall demonstrate no
change in controller PID, executable identity, cadence, display, stimulus,
relay state, or physical alarm behavior.

\subsection{Legacy GIZMo control acceptance}

Each control stage in Section~\ref{sec:legacy-control} requires a separate test
record. Threshold acceptance shall cover anonymous, wrong-credential,
wrong-type, out-of-range, stale/mismatched-state, allowed, repeated, and rollback
cases plus persistent, live-word, journal, OPC~UA, and physical-display
readback. Future stages shall additionally cover simultaneous requests,
controller crash, timeout, power interruption, audit completeness, safe-state
restoration, and rollback to the capability-bounded release. Calibration and
relay tests additionally require an approved isolated electrical setup and
independent observation of the restored HIGH~Z state.
