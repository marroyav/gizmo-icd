\begin{abstract}
This public review copy describes the interface between DUNE Slow Controls (SC) and the
Ground Impedance Monitor (GIZMo).  SC supports two hardware implementations:
GIZMo Kria, built around an AMD/Xilinx Kria K26 SOM on the KR260 Robotics
Starter Kit carrier, and legacy GIZMo, built around the ZedBoard.  Both use the
common supervisory OPC~UA contract while retaining different internal
hardware, firmware, operating-system, and control capabilities.  The document
separates the physical measurement layer, hardware-control layer, internal
application protocols, public OPC~UA interface, Ignition display and history,
Detector Protection boundary, calibration authority, and commissioning gates.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}

The GIZMo monitors the impedance relationship between detector ground and
building ground.  It applies an AC stimulus, observes the returned signal
through the installed current-transformer and analog path, calculates
impedance-related quantities, and provides a local alarm indication.  SC uses
the public machine interface for authorized configuration and operations,
operator display, alarms, and history.

The reviewed GIZMo Kria runtime (package version 0.4.5,
OPC~UA model 1.3.0) provides the native Kria endpoint.  The legacy GIZMo runs
the preserved 2017 ARM controller on its ZedBoard. SC shall support both
implementations. The adapter validates the controller against a controlled
SHA-256 fingerprint before publishing data. The fingerprint is omitted from
this public copy and retained in the commissioning record:

\begin{lstlisting}
<controlled-executable-sha256>
\end{lstlisting}

This ICD specifies interfaces and ownership; it does not replace electrical
schematics, the device software design, a Detector Protection design, or a
traceable metrology procedure.  In particular, existence of legacy calibrator
hardware and a \code{CAL} command does not establish a production self-
calibration process.

\section{System Architecture}

\subsection{Layered architecture}

\begin{lstlisting}
Layer 0  Detector ground <-> building ground / CT / saturable inductor
                           |
Layer 1  GIZMo analog front end, ADC/DAC, FPGA, relays, local display
                           |
Layer 2  Platform controller and private application protocols
         Kria: ZMon :5055, ZMQ :5555, private control socket
         Zed:  GIZMO.elf, FPGA MMIO, VM801P SPI, SysV service
                           |
Layer 3  Public interface: OPC UA Binary over UA TCP
                           |
Layer 4  Ignition: tags, controls, alarms, trends, and history
\end{lstlisting}

Only Layer~3 is the normal SC command/data boundary.  Interfaces in Layer~2
are private implementation details and shall not become independent SC control
paths.  Layer~0 and Layer~1 work is a controlled electrical or calibration
activity.

\section{Physical and Electrical Interface}

\subsection{Measurement boundary}

The installed measurement path consists of detector ground, building-ground
reference, the stimulus path, saturable-inductor assembly, current transformer
(CT), analog front end, and the associated cable shields and chassis bonding.
The system drawing places the GIZMo, CT, saturable inductor, alarm beacon, and
audio indication across the detector/building-ground monitoring boundary.

\subsection{External connectors}

The legacy rear centers labelled \code{TO DETECTOR} and \code{FROM CT} are
active stimulus/output and signal/input conductors.  A powered resistance-mode
measurement across them injects meter current into the active loop and is not
a passive detector-ground measurement.  The observed 50-kohm external-box
response is useful characterization evidence but is not a calibration point.

\subsection{Kria hardware layer}

GIZMo Kria uses an AMD/Xilinx Kria K26 SOM mounted on the KR260 Robotics
Starter Kit carrier, the custom control board, FPGA overlay, ADC/DAC signal
path, relay drivers, 5-inch EVE display, alarm/audio connections, and two
Processing-System Ethernet interfaces.  The available records establish the
K26/KR260 platform but do not establish the SOM commercial/industrial grade.
The maintained software maps the installed FPGA resources at fixed addresses;
the overlay identity and expected devices are monitored through the public
model.

\req{013} The Kria overlay, control-board revision, calibration tables, and
physical cable assignment shall form one controlled device configuration.

\subsection{Legacy hardware layer}

The legacy platform consists of a ZedBoard, 2017 FPGA bitstream, amplifier
board 176962, PMod calibrator 176971, and VM801P/FT801 local display.  The
calibrator has five populated binary-weighted sections measured during
characterization as 8.15, 16.17, 31.60, 63.52, and 127.12~ohm.  The R6
footprint is unpopulated and participates in the high-impedance disconnect
topology.  These DMM readings are as-built engineering observations, not
traceable standards.

The relay controller accepts a one-shot set/reset pulse for each latching
relay.  Finite states select combinations of R1--R5; the restored normal state
opens the chain through the unpopulated R6 position.  Any diagnostic that owns
relay MMIO shall first stop \code{GIZMO.elf}, guarantee cleanup on normal exit,
error, and interruption, verify restoration, and then restart the original
controller.

\section{Link, Transport, and Internal Protocols}

\subsection{Controls network}

The SC-facing physical network is Ethernet.  Each OPC~UA server shall use a
site-assigned address on the restricted controls network.  Final hostname,
address, route, switch port, VLAN, and firewall entries are installation data.

\subsection{Kria private interfaces}

\begin{longtable}{L{0.20\textwidth} L{0.18\textwidth} L{0.52\textwidth}}
\toprule
\textbf{Interface} & \textbf{Default} & \textbf{Purpose and policy} \\
\midrule
ZMon stream & TCP 5055 & Private measurement-engine result stream used by
the display and command bridge. \\
ZeroMQ command API & TCP 5555 & Compatibility command API for threshold,
averaging, calibration, ADC capture, time, and latch actions.  It is not the
preferred public SC interface. \\
Temperature stream & TCP 5005 & Private temperature producer. \\
SDR stream & TCP 5556 & Private raw acquisition stream; the OPC~UA snapshot is

Privileged helper & Unix socket & Root-owned, allow-listed operations including

Dashboard & HTTP 8080 & Read-only operator/engineering view. \\
\bottomrule
\end{longtable}

The package target \code{gizmo.target} is the local service-lifecycle boundary.
Systemd starts, stops, supervises, and restarts the owned components.  Service
state is monitored through OPC~UA; arbitrary \code{systemctl} execution is not
an OPC~UA capability.

\subsection{Legacy private interfaces}

The 2017 controller is launched by its SysV service in normal measurement
mode. Its command parser has native commands including
\code{RUN}, \code{CAL}, \code{set_th}, \code{read_adc}, \code{set_time},
\code{set_res}, and \code{set_rly}.  The SysV wrapper supports start, stop,
restart, and status.  These facts establish native capability; they do not by
themselves authorize remote SC control.

The Zed-to-VM801P display link is a private 16-bit SPI exchange at 4~MHz,
mode~0, MSB-first.  The controller sends a tagged threshold, tagged resistance,
and an untagged comparison value; the display returns alarm bits used by the
legacy controller.  This SPI link is not an SC interface.

The commissioning adapter reads a coherent completed \code{RUN} frame from
the exact executable through a restricted SSH path.  It shall check one PID,
the executable hash, coherent finite I/Q/magnitude/resistance, and the recovered
transfer function before publishing a new sequence.  Repeated polls of the same
frame shall not manufacture new measurements.

\section{Public OPC UA Application Interface}

\subsection{Endpoint and information model}

The public protocol is OPC~UA Binary over UA TCP, normally at:

\begin{lstlisting}
opc.tcp://<assigned-gizmo-host>:4840
\end{lstlisting}

The namespace URI is \code{urn:fnal:gizmo}.  Clients shall resolve that URI at
connection time and shall not hard-code a namespace index.  Canonical objects
are rooted at \code{Objects/GIZMo}; deterministic string NodeIds include:

\begin{lstlisting}
GIZMo.Identity.*
GIZMo.Measurement.*
GIZMo.Alarm.*
GIZMo.Configuration.*
GIZMo.Operations.*
GIZMo.Calibration.*
GIZMo.Health.*
\end{lstlisting}

Model 1.x revisions preserve NodeId, datatype, rank, and physical meaning.
A breaking change requires a new major-version namespace.  The recovered
\code{SimpleOPCUAServer/CommandObject} tree may remain for migration, but new
SC integration shall use the canonical model.

\subsection{DataValue and quality semantics}

Every variable shall provide its OPC~UA datatype, StatusCode, and source
timestamp.  Unknown numeric values shall not be exposed as plausible zero.
Before a first sample, the status is \code{BadWaitingForInitialData}; a retained
sample after loss may be \code{UncertainLastUsableValue}; an unavailable
platform feature is \code{BadNotSupported} or \code{BadDataUnavailable}.

\req{020} \code{ResistanceOhm} is a physical value only when
\code{ResistanceRange=InRange}.  HIGH~Z shall be an explicit range state with
a null/NaN resistance and non-good quality, never a fabricated 500- or
999-ohm measurement.

\req{021} \code{Alarm.Active}, when available, shall come from the device's
authoritative alarm branch.  SC, the adapter, and Ignition shall not recompute
it from the resistance and threshold readbacks.

\subsection{Monitoring groups}

The canonical model contains Identity, Measurement, Alarm, Thermal, Time,
OperatingSystem, Network, Storage, Firmware, Services, Calibration, SDR,
Configuration, Operations, and Health groups.  The two platform intake lists
are the detailed selected SC integration catalogs: they contain the
production-facing variables, controls, command readbacks, and requested model
additions defined by this ICD, rather than all native diagnostic leaves.
Unsupported legacy fields remain present only when needed for contract
compatibility and carry a non-good StatusCode.

\subsection{Numeric-field convention}

In the companion intake spreadsheets, \emph{Sensor Span} is a verified
software bound or documented presentation domain, \emph{Nominal Value} is the
requested initial value or a clearly identified current observation, and
\emph{Desired Range} is the initial normal or commissioning check band.  A
wider authorized write range, where one exists, is stated in Notes.  The
Low/High, delay, deadband, and action
columns define Ignition alarm configuration only.  A blank cell means that no
supported number or action has been established; it is not zero and it is not
``not applicable'' data copied from the intake template.  Notes distinguish
implemented bounds, current device/package values, measured observations, and
provisional commissioning choices.

Thus the system is not monitor-only: GIZMo Kria already supports configuration

\subsection{GIZMo Kria controls}

GIZMo Kria exposes two writable UInt32 configuration variables.  Their stable
NodeIds and ranges are:

\begin{lstlisting}
GIZMo.Configuration.ThresholdOhm              0 .. 1,000,000 ohm
GIZMo.Configuration.AveragesPerCalculation    1 .. 1,000,000
\end{lstlisting}

The Kria package initializes the threshold and averaging count to 100.  For
initial joint commissioning, SC shall request 100~ohm and verify the live
\code{Measurement.ThresholdOhm} readback.  This value matches the sustained
setpoint in the retained Kria history; a brief 2-kohm commissioning excursion
is test evidence and is not a desired operating value.  Although the firmware
accepts zero,
the initial Ignition operator range is 1--500~ohm because zero effectively
disables the resistance-threshold branch.  The accepted OPC~UA bounds remain
unchanged.

It exposes the following typed methods and command-result variable:

\begin{lstlisting}
GIZMo.Operations.ClearLatch()
GIZMo.Operations.StartCalibration(UInt32)
GIZMo.Operations.CaptureAdc()
GIZMo.Operations.NormalizeMagnitude()
GIZMo.Operations.SetSystemTime(DateTime)
GIZMo.Configuration.LastCommandResult
\end{lstlisting}

\req{030} SC writes and methods shall be bounded, role-authorized, logged, and
followed by readback of the resulting state.  A transport-level success alone
does not prove that the requested physical state was reached.

\req{031} Clearing the latch shall not suppress an active physical alarm.  A
device restart or reboot shall not be described as ``clear latch.''

\req{032} Restarting \code{gizmo-zmon.service} and rebooting the Kria are local
maintenance/recovery actions until separately specified as canonical OPC~UA
methods.  SC shall not infer those permissions from writable service-status
nodes; service-status nodes are read-only.

\subsection{Legacy GIZMo controlled expansion}\label{sec:legacy-control}

The first ZedBoard release is a read-only validation pilot.  Afterwards,
controls may be introduced one at a time through an allow-listed broker; SC
shall never pass arbitrary shell text or raw MMIO values to the target.

As verified on 12 August 2026, no OPC~UA server runs on the ZedBoard: the
target listens on its legacy SSH and HTTP services, while TCP port 4840 is
closed.  The preserved target also lacks the modern runtime required by the
canonical server.  On 13 August 2026 the initial monitoring-only adapter was
implemented and deployed on a controlled workstation at
\code{opc.tcp://<site-assigned-legacy-adapter>:<port>}. It pins the ZedBoard host key, requires
the controlled executable hash, and reads only a validated coherent completed
RUN frame and process identity.  All OPC~UA variables are client read-only and
all operation methods return \code{BadNotSupported}.  No historian or
production Ignition binding is enabled until the Stage~0 gate below passes.
This placement keeps the 2017 controller as the sole owner of FPGA/MMIO and
relay resources.

\begin{longtable}{L{0.16\textwidth} L{0.28\textwidth} L{0.36\textwidth}}
\toprule
\textbf{Stage} & \textbf{Candidate capability} & \textbf{Release gate} \\
\midrule
0 & Monitoring only & 100 coherent cycles match the local display; restart,
stale, wrong-hash, partial-frame, and network-loss tests pass. \\
1 & Measurement-engine restart/status & Exact target identity; serialized
service ownership; timeout; post-restart PID/hash/freshness readback; audit. \\
2 & Threshold write & Exact command grammar and numeric bounds; persisted or
restart behavior documented; new threshold returned by the controller/display;
authorization and rollback tested. \\
3 & ADC capture and clock correction & Output-file ownership, bounded transfer,
timeout, target-time quality, and restoration of normal \code{RUN} mode tested. \\
4 & Calibration sweep & Approved isolated fixture; exclusive relay ownership;
raw I/Q and state provenance; guaranteed HIGH~Z cleanup; independent validation;
no automatic activation. \\
\bottomrule
\end{longtable}

\req{033} A failed, timed-out, or interrupted legacy command shall leave the
last verified normal measurement mode or report a latched command-failure state
requiring maintenance.  It shall not report success from command submission
alone.

\req{034} Direct \code{set_res} and \code{set_rly} shall remain expert,
isolated maintenance operations.  They shall not be general-purpose Ignition
buttons or writable tag values.

\req{035} A future restart method shall name its scope explicitly, for example
\code{RestartMeasurementEngine}; a generic \code{Reset} method is prohibited.

\section{Detector Control Display, Alarms, and History}

\subsection{Display}

Ignition shall show source identity, endpoint health, sample age, measurement
quality, resistance/range, threshold, calibration state, and relevant control
capabilities.  The hardware implementation shall be labelled \emph{GIZMo Kria
(K26/KR260)} or \emph{Legacy GIZMo (ZedBoard)} wherever it could be ambiguous.
HIGH~Z is rendered as a state, not as a precise endpoint.  The local display
remains an independent commissioning reference; disagreement creates a
diagnostic event and never triggers automatic calibration.

\subsection{Sampling and freshness}

Kria measurement data update nominally each second; platform data update at
10- or 30-second cadence.  Clients should use subscriptions.  The Zed adapter
may poll each second but shall increment \code{Measurement.Sequence} and write
to Ignition history only when it detects a new coherent completed \code{RUN}
result.  A stale source becomes non-good within two expected completed cycles.

\subsection{Ignition history boundary}

Ignition shall subscribe directly to each GIZMo OPC~UA endpoint and shall own
the production tag history.  History shall preserve the reported device
identity, OPC~UA source timestamp, StatusCode, null/HIGH~Z gaps, and command
audit events.  The SC integration requires no intermediate history service
between OPC~UA and Ignition.  Ignition history failure shall not stop local
measurement or physical alarm indication.

The retained Kria SQLite replica covers 27 July through 3 August 2026 and

runtime 0.4.4; the maintained source baseline reviewed for this ICD is runtime

A read-only live check of the connected legacy board on 12 August 2026
collected 15 coherent completed \code{RUN} frames from the unchanged
\code{GIZMO.elf}.  The controller returned a 100-ohm threshold, 15
accumulations, 111.554--111.902~ohm calculated resistance, and a 112-ohm green
display.  The I, Q, magnitude, and derived \code{atan2} observations are

do not validate the missing CT/inductor path or the full detector-loop

The deployed adapter subsequently captured 27 contiguous cycles
(\code{Sequence} 21--47) on 13 August 2026.  Resistance was
111.465703--112.010978~ohm (mean 111.722419~ohm), the threshold remained
exactly 100~ohm, and the calculated legacy display-equivalent rounding was
112~ohm in 26 cycles and 111~ohm in one.  Target communication was then lost
before 100 cycles.  The captured values carried
\code{UncertainLastUsableValue} and \code{Measurement.Quality=Uncertain},
consistent with the unvalidated legacy measurement path; after the loss, the
adapter exposed \code{BadNoCommunication} and \code{Health.Overall=Failed}

therefore remain monitor/trend checks and the spreadsheet alarm cells remain

protective action shall not depend only on Linux, OPC~UA, or Ignition.

\section{Calibration and Maintenance}

\subsection{GIZMo Kria}

GIZMo Kria calibration is a controlled maintenance method, not an automatic
SC reaction.  Production tables shall be staged, validated, content-hashed,
activated atomically, and rollback-tested.  Success, error, timeout, process
termination, and power-interruption paths shall restore and verify the normal
relay state.  Calibration state and the active table identities are published
through OPC~UA.

\subsection{Legacy GIZMo}

The legacy \code{CAL N} command selects 32 R1--R5 states and logs lock-in
measurements.  It does not fit, validate, persist, or activate a transfer
function; it does not use the abandoned \code{calib2.txt}; and it does not
guarantee normal-state restoration at completion.  Starting \code{RUN}
restores the normal \code{SetRes(-1)} selection, but an interrupted calibration
can leave a finite test resistance selected.

Accordingly, the initial endpoint reports
\code{Calibration.State=CharacterizationOnly} or \code{Unvalidated}.  A future
calibration workflow requires the restored CT/saturable-inductor fixture,
measured standards, a common runtime/calibration acquisition primitive, raw I/Q
and relay-state records, up/down sweeps, blind validation, clipping and
hysteresis checks, guaranteed cleanup, atomic model activation, and rollback.
Capacitance remains unsupported until mixed R/C characterization establishes a
defensible model.

\section{Security and Access Control}

Both endpoints belong on the restricted controls network or behind approved
authenticated transport.  Final certificates, trust lists, usernames/roles,
and firewall rules are installation data jointly assigned by SC and site
cybersecurity.

GIZMo Kria currently supports an explicitly configured insecure OPC~UA policy
for an isolated network; serialization is not encryption.  Production
deployment shall document whether that exception is accepted or deploy the
site certificate and role policy.

The legacy commissioning transport shall pin the SSH host key and use a
restricted identity/command.  Unrestricted root SSH is not a production
command interface.  The control broker shall allow only typed operations with
bounds, mutual exclusion, timeout, readback, audit, and failure cleanup.  It
shall not expose arbitrary command strings, environment values, file paths,
shell execution, FPGA MMIO, or relay bit patterns.

Credentials, private keys, and unbounded log text shall not be published as
OPC~UA values or Ignition history/audit events.

\section{Test Stand, Commissioning, and Acceptance}

\subsection{Physical and common interface acceptance}

Before SC sign-off, the parties shall:

\begin{itemize}
  \item verify connector labels, power, fusing, shield/ground assignment,
  CT polarity, and the approved stimulus path;
  \item resolve the namespace URI and verify every required NodeId, datatype,
  access class, EngineeringUnits value, StatusCode, and source timestamp with a
  generic OPC~UA client;
  \item verify DeviceId, platform, runtime/ELF identity, endpoint, and time;
  \item force disconnect, stale data, restart, and out-of-range conditions and
  confirm non-good quality and last-good semantics;
  \item confirm HIGH~Z is never persisted as a fabricated numeric value;
  \item verify Ignition display, alarm journal, command audit, trends, and
  history identity separation; and
  \item demonstrate that OPC~UA or Ignition-history failure does not stop local
  measurement or physical alarm indication.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{GIZMo Kria acceptance}

All maintained runtime tests shall pass.  Threshold and averaging writes shall
enforce type and range; every method shall report a bounded result.  Latch clear,
time correction, ADC capture, magnitude normalization, and calibration shall be
tested with authorization and readback.  Calibration testing shall include
error/interruption cleanup and rollback.  A 24-hour soak and power-interruption
recovery test shall complete before production approval.

\subsection{Legacy GIZMo monitoring acceptance}

At least 100 completed cycles shall match the physical display's rounded
resistance, including identical consecutive readings.  Wrong hash, multiple or
changed PID, target restart, adapter restart, network outage, partial update,
stale frame, and non-finite values shall not produce a false fresh sample.
During this pilot, every write/method shall fail explicitly without reaching
the controller or relay hardware.  Ignition shall retain the ZedBoard samples
with their reported device identity, source timestamp, and StatusCode.

\subsection{Legacy GIZMo control acceptance}

Each control stage in Section~\ref{sec:legacy-control} requires a separate test
record.  Tests shall cover allowed and rejected values, simultaneous requests,
broker/SSH loss, controller crash, timeout, power interruption, command
readback, audit completeness, safe-state restoration, and rollback to the
monitoring-only release.  Calibration and relay tests additionally require an
approved isolated electrical setup and independent observation of the restored
HIGH~Z state.
