\section*{Contractor shift checks}
Contractor personnel work with the cryostat and beam structure connected to Building Ground. At shift start and handover, acknowledge the grounding status:

\begin{enumerate}
\item Check the Building Ground bond with a multimeter.
\item Check the knife-switch position and tagout status.
\item Clear tools and electrical cords so the FNAL I\&I/GIZMO group can test before, after, or between shifts.
\end{enumerate}
GIZMO readings, dashboard checks, and isolation tests are the responsibility of the FNAL I\&I/GIZMO group.

\section*{FNAL I\&I/GIZMO isolation tests}
Use the planned knife-switch/tagout arrangement for periodic tests of shorts, capacitance, and coupling between Detector Ground and Building Ground.

\begin{enumerate}
\item Coordinate the test window with the contractor and confirm the area is clear of tools and electrical cords.
\item Establish and record the test grounding state using the authorized switching/tagout procedure.
\item Check fresh data, measurement quality, alarm, frequency, and threshold. Record isolation, valid capacitance, and coupling diagnostics; compare with the permitted limits.
\item Restore and verify the required Building Ground connection, complete tagout closeout, and hand the structure back before contractor work resumes.
\end{enumerate}
Plan tests before, after, or between shifts, often enough to avoid disruptive late discovery of unwanted connections. Test cadence, detailed shift routine, settling time, permitted isolation limits, and failed/missed-test response: TBD. The recorded 100 \ensuremath{\Omega} alarm threshold is not an approved construction-isolation acceptance limit.

Record test time, grounding state, findings, and handback in Procore Daily Log \textgreater{} GIZMO. HIGH Z is a range indication, not a resistance value. The last stored sample had Uncertain quality, an uncertain clear alarm, and a latched alarm; it does not establish the present ground condition.

\section*{FNAL: alarm response}
\begin{enumerate}
\item Check the recorded grounding state and any intentional construction or welding bond. An isolation result is not meaningful while the required Building Ground connection is in place.
\item If the alarm is unexpected for the recorded grounding state, stop new ground-affecting work. Record the time and alarm reason; notify the activity coordinator and grounding/GIZMO contact.
\item Check what changed: welding returns, temporary leads, cable shields, piping, scaffolding, or tools. Investigate under the work plan.
\item Keep the data and record the disposition. Do not change the threshold to make the alarm disappear.
\end{enumerate}
\section*{FNAL: lost data}
Treat stale or non-Good data as unavailable monitoring. A running dashboard or an old clear alarm is not proof that GIZMO is working.

\begin{enumerate}
\item Record the last sample time and notify the GIZMO and SC/DPS contacts.
\item During an isolation test, do not accept a result without valid data or an approved alternative. During contractor work, verify the required Building Ground connection; continuation and rescheduling follow the work plan.
\item Check the instrument power and network through the responsible on-site contact. The server reports loss of reachability; the cause has not been established.
\item After connection returns, verify advancing timestamps, usable measurement/alarm quality, and resumed history before relying on GIZMO again.
\end{enumerate}
\section*{Welding}
Use the approved welding-return arrangement. The FNAL I\&I/GIZMO group manages alarm inhibition and retains alarms and history. Restore the contractor grounding state after work; the FNAL group checks isolation at the scheduled test.

\section*{FNAL: server check}
On dune-fd-test01, run:

\begin{quote}\ttfamily\small
/storage/gizmo-monitor/bin/status
\end{quote}
It reports the historian, dashboard, upstream connection, stored samples, and disk space. Check upstream connectivity and the newest GIZMO sample, not just whether the processes are running.

For a local dashboard connection:

\begin{quote}\ttfamily\small
ssh -N -L 127.0.0.1:18081:127.0.0.1:18081 dune-fd-test01
\end{quote}
Then open http://127.0.0.1:18081/ in the browser. Use the configured SSH access for the server.

\section*{FNAL: shutdown}
Coordinate any loss of monitoring before shutdown. Use SOP004 and the deployed operating procedure to disable excitation and power down. Leave the permanent safety-ground path intact. Record the time and reason.
