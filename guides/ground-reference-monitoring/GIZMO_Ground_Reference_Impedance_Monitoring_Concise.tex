\section*{Contractor shift checks}
Contractor personnel work with the cryostat and beam structure connected to Building Ground. At shift start and handover, acknowledge the grounding status:

\begin{enumerate}
\item Check the Building Ground bond with a multimeter.
\item Check the knife-switch position and tagout status.
\item Clear tools and electrical cords so the FNAL I\&I/GIZMO group can test before, after, or between shifts.
\end{enumerate}
GIZMO readings, dashboard checks, and isolation tests are the responsibility of the FNAL I\&I/GIZMO group.

\section*{FNAL I\&I/GIZMO isolation tests}
Use the planned knife-switch/tagout arrangement for periodic tests of shorts, capacitance, and coupling between Detector Ground and Building Ground.

\begin{enumerate}
\item Coordinate the test window with the contractor and confirm the area is clear of tools and electrical cords.
\item Establish and record the test grounding state using the authorized switching/tagout procedure.
\item Check fresh data, measurement quality, and frequency. Record isolation, valid capacitance, and coupling diagnostics; compare with the permitted limits.
\item Restore and verify the required Building Ground connection, complete tagout closeout, and hand the structure back before contractor work resumes.
\end{enumerate}
Plan tests before, after, or between shifts, often enough to avoid disruptive late discovery of unwanted connections. Test cadence, detailed shift routine, settling time, permitted isolation limits, and failed/missed-test response: TBD.

Record test time, grounding state, findings, and handback in Procore Daily Log \textgreater{} GIZMO. HIGH Z is a range indication, not a resistance value. The last stored sample had Uncertain quality; it does not establish the present ground condition.

\section*{Measurement availability requirement}
As of September 30, 2026, remote GIZMO measurements have been unavailable for 17 days, following loss or damage of the mine network switch. The last retained sample is September 13 at 07:11:34 UTC.

The facility must provide technical support to keep GIZMO measurements available throughout cryostat installation. Support must cover the instrument, mine network switch, communications, and measurement recovery.

When remote measurements are unavailable, the FNAL I\&I/GIZMO group must provide local measurements in their place and record the results. Contractor work must not be delayed by facility monitoring outages; the facility must maintain the local measurement alternative.

\section*{FNAL: shutdown}
Coordinate any loss of monitoring before shutdown. Use SOP004 and the deployed operating procedure to disable excitation and power down. Leave the permanent safety-ground path intact. Record the time and reason.
